How V5 evaluates

V5 calculates the paired-lot means and percent bias:

% Bias = (New lot mean − Current lot mean) / Current lot mean × 100

You set the acceptance limit. The software records the result and disposition for the audit trail.

The acceptance limit must come from your laboratory's approved validation/SOP criteria; the default shown here is only a placeholder.
