How external access works

  1. Create a secure link here.
  2. Copy the HTTPS URL and send it to a provider/clinic.
  3. The provider opens only the external requisition page. No LIS login is required.
  4. The submitted order appears in your protected LabOS Orders queue.
  5. Internal pages, prior patient records, finance, QC, user management, and other clinics remain inaccessible through the public link.
