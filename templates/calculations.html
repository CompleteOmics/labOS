Built-in Clinical Calculations

BMI

BMI = weight(lb) / height(in)^2 × 703

LabOS calculates BMI automatically when height and weight are entered.

eGFR: CKD-EPI 2021 creatinine, race-free

eGFR = 142 × min(Scr/κ,1)^α × max(Scr/κ,1)^−1.200 × 0.9938^Age × 1.012 [if female]

Scr in mg/dL; κ=0.7 female or 0.9 male; α=−0.241 female or −0.302 male. LabOS calculates this for adults age 18+ when DOB, sex, and serum creatinine are available. Use IDMS-traceable creatinine and validate locally before clinical reporting.
