Policy design

V5 separates laboratory policy from hard-coded software logic. That means the director can enable or disable QC rules and result-review holds without editing source code.

Autoverification guardrails currently include: critical-value holds, latest-QC failure holds, and configurable delta checks against a patient's previous released numeric result.

These are intentionally conservative. They do not replace method validation, SOP approval, competency, medical review, or regulatory requirements.
