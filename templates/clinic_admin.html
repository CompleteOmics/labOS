Access model

Providers: see orders belonging to their assigned clinic, including orders created by other providers in that clinic.

Complete Omics staff/directors: see the full laboratory queue across clinics.

Share links: can be bound to one clinic so orders submitted from that link are automatically assigned to the correct clinic.

Use individual accounts for each physician and staff member. Do not share provider passwords across a practice.
